About AirBrush

Digital art has long depended on hardware. Styluses and drawing tablets cost thousands of rupees, most drawing software must be installed before it can be used, and the way we create on a screen has barely changed since the mouse.

What if your hand were the drawing tool?

AirBrush is a browser-based creative platform that answers that question. It follows your hand through the webcam so that you can draw in mid-air, lets you describe your idea by voice or in writing, and turns your drawing into finished artwork with artificial intelligence. There is nothing to download, no special equipment to buy, and no cost to use.

How it works

  1. I. Open your webcam

    The camera tracks 21 points on your hand in real time. Everything is processed in your browser, and no special hardware is required.

  2. II. Draw in the air

    Raise one finger to paint, two fingers to erase, and an open palm to pause. Strokes appear on the canvas as you move.

  3. III. Generate with AI

    Describe what you have drawn, in words or by voice, choose a style, and receive finished artwork. Save your drawing and the result together in your gallery.

For the best results

  • Sit in a well-lit room. Hand tracking becomes less accurate in low light.
  • Use a plain, uncluttered background behind your hand.
  • Keep your whole hand inside the webcam frame, about an arm's length from the screen.
  • Use Chrome, Edge or Safari. Voice input depends on browser support.

Brush Up

A first-time guide to drawing in the air. Read it once and you will know every control in AirBrush.

Before you begin

  • Use Chrome, Edge or Safari on a computer with a webcam.
  • When the browser asks, choose Allow for the camera. The microphone is needed only if you want to describe your drawing by voice.
  • Sit in a well-lit room, with a plain background behind you and your hand about an arm's length from the screen.

1. Raise your hand

Open the Draw tab and raise one hand in front of the webcam, with your palm toward the camera and your fingers visible inside the Webcam box. A fine skeleton of coloured lines and joints appears over your hand. This means the camera can see it.

A small circle follows your fingertip in both the Webcam box and the Canvas box. It is your cursor. The word at the bottom right of the page shows what the app is doing: Ready, Drawing, Erasing or Paused.

2. The three gesture rules

GestureHow to make itWhat happens
Index finger aloneRaise only your index finger and fold the others.Draw. The cursor takes the colour of your brush, and your fingertip paints on the canvas.
Index and middle fingerRaise those two fingers together and fold the other two.Erase. The cursor turns red and removes what it touches. The eraser is wider than the brush.
Whole palmOpen your hand with all four fingers raised.Nothing. Drawing pauses. Use it to rest or to move your hand without leaving a mark.

Any other hand shape, such as a fist or three fingers, is ignored and the app simply shows Ready. Only the two gestures above ever change the canvas.

3. Your first drawing, step by step

  1. Choose a colour and a size from the ribbon at the top (see the next section).
  2. Raise your hand, then show your palm. The app pauses, so nothing is drawn while you get ready.
  3. Fold all fingers except your index finger. Move it slowly and the line appears on the Canvas box. The webcam works like a mirror, so move your hand the way you would in front of a mirror.
  4. To move to a new spot without drawing, show your palm, reposition, then raise your index finger again.
  5. To fix a mistake, raise your index and middle finger together and move over it, or press Undo.
  6. When you are happy, press Done drawing.

4. The ribbon, control by control

Undo
Removes your most recent stroke. Press it again to remove the one before.
Clear
Wipes the whole canvas after you confirm. This cannot be undone.
Size
A slider from 1 to 10. It applies from your next stroke and also changes how wide the eraser is.
Colour swatches
Click any of the fifteen squares to paint in that colour. Strokes you have already drawn keep their own colours.
Custom color
Opens a colour wheel. Click the wheel to pick a hue, slide Brightness to make it lighter or darker, or type a hex code such as #ee3b45 in the box. Press Esc to close it.
Drawing status
Reads Drawing active when your hand can paint and Drawing paused when it cannot, for example after Done drawing or while you are on another tab.
Done drawing
Saves your sketch and opens the next stage. You need at least one stroke first.
Resume drawing
Brings you back to the same canvas, colour and size so that you can keep drawing.

5. After you finish drawing

  1. Your drawing appears on the left. On the right, you can name it in What have you drawn? This is optional, but it helps the image match your shapes and colours.
  2. In Would you like anything added? you can ask for extras, such as "add a red apple on the tree". This is also optional.
  3. Prefer to speak? Press the microphone button next to either box, allow the microphone, and say your description.
  4. Choose an output style: realistic, black and white, watercolour, oil painting, pencil sketch, cartoon, anime, pixel art, 3D render or comic book.
  5. Press Generate image and wait. If a message says something went wrong, simply press it again.
  6. Press Add to gallery to keep the result.

6. The Art gallery

Each saved piece shows your drawing and the generated image side by side. Use Download to save either one to your computer, or Delete to remove the piece. The gallery is stored in this browser only, so clearing the site's data or switching browsers will remove it from view.

If something does not work

The hand is not detected
Add light, use a plainer background, and keep your whole hand inside the Webcam box.
Nothing is drawn
Check that the status says Drawing active. If it says paused, press Resume drawing. Also check that you are showing only your index finger and not a palm.
Stray marks appear
Show your palm before you move your hand to a new place.
The camera is blocked
Click the camera icon in the browser's address bar, allow access, and reload the page.
Voice does not work
Use Chrome, Edge or Safari, and allow the microphone when asked.
